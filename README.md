# 📦 Food Delivery Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 🚀 Overview
This project features an interactive **Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze food delivery operational performance, customer purchasing habits, payment channel preferences, and delivery success rates. It provides actionable insights for business stakeholders to optimize menu offerings and streamline delivery logistics.

## 📊 Key Features & Visuals
* **KPI Summary Cards:** Displays core metrics including Total Quantity (15,103), Total Transactions (2,746), and Average Quantity per Transaction (5.50)[cite: 1].
* **Monthly Sales Trends:** Column and line charts tracking quantity and transaction distribution across months, peaking in February with 792 transactions[cite: 1].
* **Customer Segmentation Matrix:** Compares Gold vs. Regular member transactions across categories like Appetizers, Beverages, Main Courses, and Snacks[cite: 1].
* **Top-Selling Items:** Horizontal bar chart highlighting top-performing food items led by Samosa (2,427) and Butter Chicken (2,044)[cite: 1].
* **Operational Performance:** Donut chart illustrating delivery status success rate, showing 94.25% delivered and 5.75% cancelled orders[cite: 1].
* **Payment Preferences:** Breakdown of transactions across UPI (1,719), Cash on Delivery (750), and Card (277) methods[cite: 1].
* **Interactive Filtering:** Slicer panel allowing dynamic filtering by Month, Member Type, Payment Method, and Restaurant Type[cite: 1].
